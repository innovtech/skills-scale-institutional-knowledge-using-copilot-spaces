# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Docs! This collection centralizes our team's project management processes, roles, templates, and checklists to ensure smooth and consistent delivery. Use this README as a starting point to navigate the core practices and artifacts.

## Overview of Project Management Processes
OctoAcme's project management approach centers on iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data‑informed decisions across a structured lifecycle of initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. New initiatives begin with a lightweight but rigorous Project One‑pager that clarifies problem, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and risks, ensuring early alignment and a clear go/no‑go decision for planning. Planning then transforms approved ideas into an actionable backlog, a release plan, milestones, and a Definition of Done, with explicit attention to dependencies and a maintained risk register to anticipate and manage uncertainty.

Execution emphasizes disciplined day‑to‑day workflows and transparent tracking. Teams work from a project board with well‑defined columns (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done), and follow a small, high‑signal pull request workflow that ties changes to issues and acceptance criteria, runs automated tests and linting in CI, and requires at least one approval before merging. Rhythm and visibility are maintained through daily standups for progress and blockers, weekly delivery syncs for demos and risks, and consistent reporting on velocity, burndown, and success metrics. Clear escalation pathways ensure blockers are rapidly triaged, elevated to product leadership when needed, and surfaced to sponsors for business‑impacting issues.

Roles and personas are explicit to reinforce accountability and effective collaboration. Project Managers orchestrate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize backlogs, and measure impact; Developers implement features, contribute to design and testing, and uphold quality; QA/Testing validate acceptance criteria and readiness; and Stakeholders provide inputs and approvals. This role clarity supports repeatable planning, efficient execution, and timely decision‑making, with an emphasis on psychological safety to encourage feedback and continuous improvement.

Quality assurance and communication are built into every phase. QA practices include unit tests for new logic, integration tests where applicable, end‑to‑end smoke tests before release, security scanning in CI, and targeted manual QA where needed. Releases follow a standardized checklist for pre‑release readiness, staged deployments, post‑deploy verification, and stakeholder announcements, with a clear rollback and incident playbook. Communication cadences include weekly PM–PdM syncs, regular standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and templated status and incident communications—ensuring a single source of truth, proactive risk visibility, and a culture of retrospective‑driven learning.
